Drug Development Fails Too Late

Most drug failures are not random. Many liabilities are already embedded in the structure of the molecule or missed by disease models that do not properly reflect the target biology. Too often, those risks surface only after years of development and millions of dollars at risk.

~90% Failure Rate

Drug candidates often advance before teams understand the liabilities most likely to break them later in the clinic. ReneuBio identifies those risks earlier.

Aging-Biology Gap

Most diseases emerge in aged tissue, but discovery often relies on models that miss aging-relevant biology. ReneuBio brings aged biology into how risk is interpreted.

Wasted Capital

Failure becomes more expensive at every stage. ReneuBio helps teams decide what to advance, redesign, prioritize, or stop before avoidable costs compound.

The Platform

AlphaForge: Predict, Explain, Redesign

AlphaForge is ReneuBio’s clinical risk prediction and molecular redesign engine for small-molecule programs.

It evaluates molecules in the context of structure, biology, and disease setting to answer three practical questions:
Is this molecule likely to fail? Why? What should we do next?

Clinical Risk Prediction

Assess failure risk before costly development decisions across safety, efficacy, durability, and translational fit.

Explainable Liability Detection

Move beyond black-box scoring. AlphaForge identifies structural and biological drivers of risk, giving teams a clearer basis for decisions.

Liability-Guided Redesign

When chemistry is the constraint, AlphaForge guides targeted redesign around key liabilities to generate lower-risk molecules.
